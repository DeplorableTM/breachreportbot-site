Built for squads who keep score

Breach Report is a Rainbow Six Siege stats bot for Discord that tracks Ranked, Quick Match, and Unranked play on PC and console. Every player links their own Ubisoft account, and the bot handles the rest — kills, deaths, wins, headshots, clutches, and more, posted straight to your server as it happens.

Multi-mode tracking

Ranked, Quick Match, and Unranked tracked independently — plus a Combined view that rolls them all together.

Live leaderboards

Nightly, monthly, season, and all-time lifetime leaderboards post automatically as your server plays.

Records that matter

Personal bests and all-time server records, broken and announced the moment they happen.

Custom scoring

Tune point values for kills, deaths, headshots, clutches, aces, and more to match how your server plays.

Session recaps

Every session ends with a full summary — who played, what they did, and how it stacked up.

Questions

What is Breach Report?

A Discord bot that automatically tracks your squad's Rainbow Six Siege stats and posts leaderboards, records, and session recaps.

Does it work on console?

Yes — Breach Report tracks Xbox and PlayStation players in addition to PC.

Which game modes does it track?

Ranked, Quick Match, and Unranked, tracked independently, plus a Combined view that adds them together.

Do I need admin permissions to use it?

No — an admin invites the bot once, then every player links their own account with /addplayer.

How much does it cost?

$3.99/month per linked account. More bundle sizes are coming soon.
